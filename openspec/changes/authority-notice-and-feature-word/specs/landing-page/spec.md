## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The landing limits Kaiord's pick to health data

Every landing sentence that says Kaiord picks which platform decides SHALL say it of health data, and SHALL say that activities, planned sessions and workouts are kept from every source, in English and Spanish. Each SHALL be proven by tests that fail if the rule stopped picking one Platform for a kind of health data or stopped keeping every source for those three kinds.

#### Scenario: The Connections section

- **WHEN** the landing's Connections section is read
- **THEN** it SHALL say "Kaiord picks which platform decides your health data, unless you already chose."
- **AND** that activities, planned sessions and workouts are kept from every source
