The chat's action confirmation card now labels all six actions and describes each one in words (in English and Spanish) instead of showing the raw tool input as JSON. A Garmin push names the workout, with its duration and date, looked up in the active athlete's library. Garmin pushes and coaching syncs say that they reach outside the device. A new workout shows its sport and a logged meal its slot. When the card cannot read what the assistant sent, Approve is disabled so nothing is approved blind; Decline still works.
