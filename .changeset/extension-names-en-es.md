Each extension is named Kaiord for its Platform (Kaiord for Garmin, Kaiord for Train2Go, Kaiord for Tanita, Kaiord for TrainingPeaks, Kaiord for WHOOP), the same in English and Spanish, and speaks the browser's language: the popup, its Connection notice, its outage date, the store name and the description are in Spanish when Chrome is in Spanish and in English otherwise. The store listings carry a Spanish description. The Kaiord App's settings, the privacy policy and the docs no longer call an extension "bridge" or "the Kaiord extension".
