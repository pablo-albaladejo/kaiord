Athletes are managed on a new page at `/athletes`, which replaces the profile manager dialog: list, create, rename in place, make active, delete behind a confirmation, and import or export one athlete. "Manage athletes…" in the account menu and the Athlete page's empty state lead there, the athlete's name is renamed in place on `/athlete`, and personal data is edited in its Body section. The copy says "athlete" instead of "profile", the privacy text names every connection, and the AI usage empty state says what fills it.
