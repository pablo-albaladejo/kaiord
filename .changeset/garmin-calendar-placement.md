Send to Garmin now places the workout on its date in the Garmin Connect calendar, not only in the library. Moving the workout moves its calendar entry, and an entry Kaiord placed earlier is deleted only after the new one has been seen on Garmin, so a slow or skewed device can leave a duplicate but never an empty day. When a write's outcome is unknown the editor says so, waits out the window in which it could still land, and then lets the athlete answer "It's in Garmin" or "Send anyway". Duplicates left behind can be dismissed once the athlete has removed them.
