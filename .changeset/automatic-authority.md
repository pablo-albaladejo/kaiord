Kaiord picks which Platform decides a kind of data by itself when more than one reports it, so the athlete is never asked: a value they enter themselves wins its day, then a scale decides weight and body composition and what is worn overnight decides sleep, HRV and the rest; Activities and planned sessions keep every source. Nothing is deleted or hidden: when that Platform disconnects the next one decides, and its own values still show on days nobody else reported. Choosing by hand is now an advanced Capability, "Choose which Platform decides", off by default: on, its choices override Kaiord's; off, they stop applying and are kept for next time. An athlete who had already chosen finds it on. The Train2Go conflict dialog is gone: Kaiord keeps the athlete's numbers and offers once to use Train2Go's instead. In English and Spanish.
