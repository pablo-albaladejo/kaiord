Move coach sessions from the calendar: drag a matched session in the Grid, or use Move to another day in the coaching dialog. Every move, the workout detail and the coach-move notice's Move back included, first asks when the target day already holds a session of the same sport. A moved session shows the day the coach had it on, and the coach-move notice now names the days a coach move replaced, each with Move back.
