Moving a session now moves it everywhere. The workout's date is the session's effective date: the athlete's move, the coach's move and the cloud merge re-date the session match with it, so a coach-moved card now moves on the calendar. Cards already stuck on a stale day are healed on the first launch and after every cloud import. A done session (with a recording, or completed by the coach) can't be moved and says why. A plan matched in another week no longer shows a second card, and a recording linked to one session is never linked to another.
