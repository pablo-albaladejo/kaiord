Capabilities: a part of the App beyond the core path stays off and out of sight until the athlete turns it on in Settings, Capabilities, and Templates is the first. For a new athlete Templates leaves no trace: no Library in the header or the bottom bar, no "Keep in library", no "Template" tile when creating a workout, no setup step for it, and the Library's address opens the Day. Turning it on brings all of it back with its templates; turning it off hides it again and deletes nothing. An athlete who already kept templates finds Templates on after the update. The decision belongs to the athlete: it syncs between their devices with Drive and travels in a backup file. In English and Spanish.
