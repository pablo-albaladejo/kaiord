Kaiord for Train2Go's popup again reads this week's plan from Train2Go at most once every 5 minutes; Refresh still reads it again. The week it read is kept only in the extension's memory, never on disk, and never reaches the Kaiord App. The privacy policy, the store listing and the privacy justification say so.
