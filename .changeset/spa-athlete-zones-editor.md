Thresholds are edited in place on the Athlete page: FTP, LTHR, threshold pace (CSS for swimming) and the maximum heart rate save on blur or Enter and say "Saved", with no dialog. The zone editor is its own page, `/athlete/zones/:sport`, reached from "Adjust zones"; its sport tabs are part of the address, so a reload or a shared link keeps the sport. The Profile Manager no longer has a zones tab, and the Athlete tagline only names sports with a threshold.
